mesocycle

Structure that
prevents plateaus

Training is organized into repeating 4-week blocks called mesocycles. Each week has a different purpose.

Week 1

On-Ramp Phase

Find your working weights with room to spare. Let your body get used to the load before things ramp up.

Week 2

Accumulation Phase

Weights go up, reps come down slightly. You're training closer to your limit and building toward peak performance.

Week 3

Intensification Phase

Your heaviest week. Weight is at its highest, reps are low, and you're pushing close to failure.

Week 4

Deload Phase

Volume drops by half and effort eases back. Your body locks in the gains and gets ready for the next cycle.
