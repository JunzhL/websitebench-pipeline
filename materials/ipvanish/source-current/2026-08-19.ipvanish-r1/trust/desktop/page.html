IPVanish Trust Center

At IPVanish, protecting your privacy is our top priority. Our users rely on us to safeguard their data using a powerful blend of cutting-edge hardware, innovative software, no-log policies, and the expertise of our dedicated team. Here’s how we continue to earn that trust.

Periodic no-log reviews

We’re dedicated to transparency and accountability, with a commitment to in-depth third-party analysis of our service. IPVanish is among the few providers that have taken proactive measures to prove through independent auditing that your information is safe while tunneling through our infrastructure.

IPVanish does not log:

  • The content of your internet traffic or the locations of your online destinations
  • Your search histories and the websites you log into
  • The apps you have running, actively or in the background
  • Your content or the destinations of your upload and download activity
  • The content or source of the online streams you’re watching

Here’s a look at the independent audits and external reviews we’ve undergone to validate our no-logs infrastructure and policies:

  • Independent audit by Schellman of our no-logs policy compliance (April 2025)
  • An audit by Leviathan Security of our no-traffic-logs privacy policy compliance (April 2022)

Customers can access the full independent privacy audit report from the “Security Reports” section of the My IPVanish Account Portal; members of the media can request the full report by emailing pr@ipvanish.com

Transparency report

Our quarterly transparency report gives insight into the requests for data our legal team handles.

Though we receive these requests daily, our strict no-logs policy generally means nothing for us to share. We do not store any data related to your online activity or service usage, including browsing history, traffic destination, metadata, DNS queries, or IPVanish-provided IP addresses.

Since this data doesn’t exist, we cannot provide it, regardless of who requests it or why. By sharing more details about the requests we receive, we strive to offer greater transparency into the steps we take to protect our users.

Security methodology at IPVanish

At IPVanish, we’re committed to protecting your privacy and securing your data. Our comprehensive data protection approach includes advanced technology, continuous monitoring, strict access controls, and employee training. Here’s how we safeguard your information.

1. Strong Security Foundations

Encryption and Access Control

We encrypt all data, both in transit and at rest, using industry-standard protocols. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for internal system access, and we ensure strict role-based access to minimize exposure.

Endpoint Protection for All Devices

All employee workstations and all systems across our development, testing, and production environments are equipped with advanced endpoint protection software. This software continuously scans for threats, detects suspicious activity, and prevents malicious attacks in real time.

System Hardening and Network Protection

Every system connected to our network undergoes thorough security hardening. This includes disabling unnecessary features, applying updates, and following strict configuration standards to reduce vulnerabilities.

Table Stakes Security

We follow essential security practices across the organization. This includes using strong passwords, regular password rotation, database and data encryption, and ensuring all data is encrypted in transit. MFA is mandatory for all systems that support it, and we maintain tight access control.

2. Proactive Threat Management

24/7 Threat Monitoring

Our Security Operations Center (SOC) monitors our systems around the clock using advanced detection tools. Suspicious activity is quickly flagged, and our InfoSec team investigates and responds in real time.

Next-Gen Endpoint Protection

Our endpoint protection tools don’t just detect known malware — they continuously monitor system behavior. If unusual activity is detected, processes are automatically terminated to prevent further damage. Data from these systems is sent to a central repository for further analysis and reporting.

Incident Response Plan

We maintain a detailed incident response plan to ensure fast action in the event of a security incident. This plan outlines roles, responsibilities, and clear escalation steps to minimize disruptions and resolve issues quickly.

3. Continuous Vulnerability Management

Regular Scanning and Ethical Hacking

We use third-party services to run regular vulnerability scans on our systems and corporate networks. Additionally, we conduct independent penetration tests and ethical hacking exercises to identify and resolve weaknesses.

Internal Security Testing

Our Application Security team performs regular “white hat” ethical hacking exercises. These internal assessments mimic real-world attacks to identify and fix potential vulnerabilities.

Automated Reporting and Analysis

All vulnerability and threat data is sent to a central repository, allowing our team to track, analyze, and address security issues quickly.

4. Privacy and Data Protection

Minimal Data Collection

IPVanish follows a privacy-first approach, collecting only the data necessary to provide our services. As an independently verified no-logs VPN provider, we have been certified that we do not collect, store, or monitor your VPN browsing activity, ensuring your privacy is always protected.

Compliance with Regulations

We comply with data privacy regulations like GDPR and CCPA. Our dedicated Data Privacy Officer ensures all data is handled appropriately, and we provide transparent, automated processes for managing data requests.

5. Employee Security Awareness

Ongoing Training

All IPVanish employees complete regular security training to recognize and respond to threats like phishing, ransomware, and social engineering. We also conduct simulated phishing tests to keep our teams sharp and ensure readiness.

Employee Onboarding and Offboarding

We use automated tools to manage employee access securely. During onboarding, new employees receive role-based access to the systems they need. When an employee leaves, access is immediately revoked to prevent unauthorized access.

6. Simplified and Secure Infrastructure

Trusted SaaS Providers

We partner with third-party Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) providers to manage platforms such as CRM systems, financial software, HR tools, and communication platforms. These platforms support both internal and external use, helping to reduce operational complexity and security risks. Each provider undergoes a rigorous security evaluation against our standards before being approved.

Automated Updates and Patching

Our systems receive automatic security updates to apply security patches as soon as they become available that quickly address vulnerabilities. Frequent updates ensure that our infrastructure remains resilient against emerging threats.

RAM-Only Servers

IPVanish is actively transitioning its network to RAM-only servers, which store data exclusively in volatile memory rather than traditional hard drives. Because no data persists once a server is powered off or rebooted, there’s nothing for third parties to retrieve from physical hardware. This infrastructure also allows for faster upgrades and improved reliability, keeping your connection both secure and seamless. Our full transition to a 100% RAM-only server fleet is planned for completion in 2027.

Our Commitment to Your Security

At IPVanish, security isn’t just a priority — it’s the foundation of everything we do. With proactive monitoring, strong encryption, and a culture of security awareness, you can trust us to keep your data safe and private every time you connect.

Industry leadership

We already hold ourselves to high standards, but our commitment to a safer, more private internet goes beyond our own practices. That’s why we collaborate with the broader VPN industry to raise the bar and enhance user protection.

As a co-founder of the VPN Trust Initiative (VTI) alongside other leading VPN providers and trade association members at the Internet Infrastructure Coalition (i2Coalition), we contribute to industry-wide advocacy and awareness efforts. The VTI has also established the VTI Principles — a set of guidelines promoting responsible practices in security, privacy, transparency, and more.
