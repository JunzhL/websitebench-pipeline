What comes with Cloud Storage?

A physical hard drive could be instantly compromised if lost, stolen, corrupted, or damaged. With Livedrive, you can back up multiple devices and access key folders from anywhere. When you choose an IPVanish Advanced Plan with Cloud Storage powered by Livedrive, you’ll unlock powerful features that enhance your file organization and data management.

Cloud Backup

Automatically save an exact copy of key files across devices with 1TB of storage in the Cloud. 

  • Create data backups of 1 computer and 5 mobile devices
  • Files are backed up continously as you edit them or at scheduled times
  • Rest easy knowing deleted data is retained for 30 days
  • Unlimited mobile backups 

File Sync & Share

Offload large files from your local machine to your Briefcase in the cloud for access and transfers anytime, anywhere. 

  • 1 TB of storage across synced devices
  • Protected file sharing with friends
  • Option to store locally as a copy or offload to the cloud
  • Unlimited device connections for secure and easy sharing, transfers, and real-time collaborations
  • Continuous syncing of files

Cloud Storage Benefits as a VPN User

Safety and
Public Wi-Fi

Our VPN does a great job of protecting your network data from being read by third parties, which is why so many customers who use public Wi-Fi trust our service. However, user error or network failures happen, especially if you don’t have the Kill Switch activated. This can potentially expose your unencrypted traffic and enable hackers to infiltrate your accounts, wreaking havoc by installing malware and deleting files. With a Cloud Storage solution, you don’t have to worry about losing your files forever in this event.

Accommodating
Frequent Travel

While traveling, physical space in your suitcase is a factor to consider. Do you have room to carry your device (especially a PC) and several external storage solutions? Customs fees can add up while traveling abroad with bulky and expensive devices, so consolidation is critical. Many of our customers are also frequent travelers, so we want to offer a way to access your essential files from anywhere. When connected to our VPN, you’ll have an added layer of security while accessing your data with File Sync & Share.

Easily Restore
Lost Files

When you constantly edit, move, and delete files on a specific device, your storage may become fragmented. Your Cloud Storage will automatically sync the files on your device for up to 30 days after a change is made. In the case of an emergency, such as a hacked or damaged device, Backup is a reliable solution to help you quickly recover your files. You don’t need to manually update your backup to include the files you want either; Livedrive Cloud Storage makes it effortless to access and restore your files whenever needed.

Cloud Storage: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

We understand that secure Cloud Storage is different from typical VPN benefits. We’re here to explain how it works and the practical benefits of this new product.

1. What is Cloud Storage?

With Cloud Storage by Livedrive, you are able to save files onto a remote server with 1TB of capacity. Cloud Storage is essentially an external hard drive that automatically copies your most important photos, documents, and media to the cloud. The IPVanish Advanced plan provisions a Cloud Storage which includes a Livedrive account that includes both Backup and Briefcase features.

2. Is Cloud Storage safe?

Yes. With Cloud Storage by Livedrive, advanced TLS encryption, 24/7 intrusion prevention, and detection network monitoring are used to make sure your data is safe. All Livedrive data centers are ISO 27001 certified, which is the highest standard of data security in the industry. Your files will also be distributed and obfuscated, meaning it is strategically split between different systems, and masked so it can’t be linked to a specific account. These are all ideal ways to safeguard your important documents, and it’s even safer than keeping your documents directly on your device, as it could fail at any moment.

3. Does Cloud Storage free up space on my device?

It can. Cloud Storage powered by Livedrive is meant to reflect the files you currently have on your device. If you modify or delete any files, they will also be permanently deleted or changed in your Cloud Backup after 30 days. However, with File Sync & Share (in your Livedrive Briefcase), you can utilize 1TB of Cloud Storage to free space on your local drive and offload large files from your devices. Make sure you remain organized with your files to ensure you don’t accidentally delete any.

4. Do I have to use Livedrive with IPVanish? 

Livedrive is a separate application and does not require IPVanish VPN to run, nor is it integrated with the IPVanish application. An IPVanish Advanced plan provisions one Livedrive account that can be activated through the IPVanish Account Portal. 

5. I already have Livedrive. How can I connect my accounts?

Existing Livedrive users can contact the IPVanish Support Team for special offers and assistance connecting their Livedrive account to their IPVanish subscription.
