What is Threat Protection Pro?

Threat Protection Pro is a built-in anti-malware solution that protects your device in real time as you browse and download. It automatically checks your activity and stops potential dangers before they can cause harm, even without an active VPN connection. 

Smarter security, built for everyday use

Explore what you can do with IPVanish Threat Protection Pro.

Stay ahead of phishing, scams, and harmful sites.

Advanced browsing security
Every page you visit is evaluated in real time, blocking dangerous content before it can load. From deceptive phishing pages to sites known for fraud, threats are stopped automatically—so you can browse more freely without second-guessing where you click.

Keep malicious downloads off your device.

Anti-malware and antivirus 
Files are checked as they’re downloaded and before they’re opened, helping prevent malware from reaching your system. If something is identified as a threat, it’s stopped and quarantined before it can run, protecting your device without requiring manual scans.

Free yourself from ads and trackers.

Tracker and ad blocker
Cut down intrusive ads and limit how much data is collected about you while you browse. By blocking trackers and unnecessary content, your experience becomes faster, cleaner, and more private. 

Stop threats before they happen.

Network tracking protection
Suspicious activity is monitored in real time to help detect and block network tracking. By identifying unusual background behavior, potential tracking threats can be stopped before they can cause harm.

How to use Threat Protection Pro

Learn how to control Threat Protection Pro on your Windows or Mac computer.

Windows

1. Open the IPVanish app

Download the IPVanish VPN app for Windows and log in.

2. Go to Threat Protection Pro

Select the shield icon in the left menu (fourth from the top).

3. Adjust your settings

All protections are enabled by default—use the Settings panel to adjust them.

How does Threat Protection Pro work?

When a threat is detected, it blocks access to malicious websites, prevents harmful downloads, and stops ads and trackers from loading. This helps reduce exposure to phishing attempts, malware, and unwanted tracking without interrupting your experience.

IPVanish Threat Protection Pro works in real time to help stop online threats before they reach your device. As you browse and download files, it checks domains, webpages, and activity against continuously updated threat intelligence to identify and block risks.

Files downloaded from the internet are scanned before you open them, helping prevent harmful software from running on your system. If a file is flagged as suspicious, it’s isolated in Quarantine so it cannot cause harm while you review it.

All blocked threats are visible within the IPVanish app, including ads, trackers, malicious domains, and unsafe downloads. You can customize your protection by enabling features and using an allow list to approve trusted sites or files.

Frequently asked questions

Does IPVanish have an antivirus or anti-malware tool?

IPVanish Threat Protection Pro includes advanced anti-malware and antivirus solutions that help block malicious downloads, unsafe websites, and online threats in real time. It’s designed to prevent online threats before they reach your device and complement your overall security setup.

What antivirus engine is Threat Protection Pro using?

Threat Protection Pro is powered by VIPRE’s threat intelligence engine, backed by decades of cybersecurity expertise. It uses real-time analysis and continuously updated threat data to help detect and block emerging risks.

Is IPVanish Threat Protection Pro worth it?

Yes, if you want more than just a VPN. Threat Protection Pro adds a strong layer of security by blocking malicious downloads, sites, and web content, helping keep your device and data safer while you browse.

What’s the difference between Threat Protection and Threat Protection Pro?

Threat Protection is available in every IPVanish app and plan type, filtering ads and malicious domains at the DNS level when an active VPN connection is established.
Threat Protection Pro, available only in the IPVanish Advanced plan in the Windows and Mac apps, adds real-time detection, including file scanning and malicious download blocking, and works even without a VPN connection.

Does Threat Protection Pro protect against new threats?

Yes. It uses real-time threat intelligence and behavioral analysis to help detect and block both known and emerging threats.

What happens when Threat Protection Pro detects a threat?

Threats are blocked automatically to prevent them from reaching your device. Suspicious files may be moved to Quarantine, a secure area where they’re isolated to prevent harm. You can review these files at any time and choose to remove or restore them.
If a website is flagged as malicious, access is blocked by default, but you can safely review it using IPVanish Secure Browser, which opens pages in a sandboxed virtual browser.

Can I choose what gets blocked?

Yes. You can use an allow list to approve trusted websites or files, giving you more control over your protection.

What’s the difference between Threat Protection Pro and IPVanish Secure Browser™?

Threat Protection Pro is an always-on protection layer that works across your online activity to block malicious websites, scan downloads, and stop trackers in real time. IPVanish Secure Browser™ is a separate, isolated browsing environment that runs in a sandbox and leaves no trace on your device after each session.
While Threat Protection Pro blocks unsafe sites by default, Secure Browser gives you a way to open and review those pages safely in isolation.
